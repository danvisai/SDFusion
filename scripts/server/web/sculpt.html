⑥ Sketch relief experimental
This is a sketch tool, not a paint bucket: drag out a rough, vague shape (as many strokes/colors as you like) on the MIDDLE of a wall — the stroke is rectified onto that wall's plane, the model composes a bas-relief for the flat patch (a dedicated scribble ControlNet reads the SHAPE you drew), and the result is sculpted into the SDF as an actual raised/carved relief. Strokes across a corner or roofline get clipped to the dominant wall. Reliefs stack: each new stroke sculpts on top of the previous result (editing the massing or details resets them). Orbit is disabled while sketching. Click Generate relief when your sketch is ready — drawing itself never submits.
Precise = the model closely follows the shape you drew ("adjust it to the right thing"). Creative = it treats your shape as loose inspiration for the motif named in the prompt (verified 2026-07-07 v2: a vague blob + "a lion head" at the creative end came back as an actual lion-head relief in the blob's place) — a clear, intentional shape (like a star) usually survives down to "balanced".
Low = keeps the colors you picked; high = AI chooses its own colors and adds real 3D shading (needed for it to read as carved/architectural, not a flat sticker).
