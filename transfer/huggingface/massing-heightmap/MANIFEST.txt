WHAT THESE MODELS CANNOT DO
  * Anything 3-D. The output is a 64x64 height map: one height per plan column. Courtyards,
    passages, arcades and overhangs are not representable. #10 measured that the LoD2 corpus at
    64^3 contains none of them, which is why the representation is sufficient FOR THIS CORPUS and
    is not a claim about architecture.
  * Detail. This is massing only, above the detail scale s* (ADR 0004).
  * A symbolic edit program. The output is geometry, not a recipe, so it does not by itself carry
    this project's editability claim.
